# Expo UI Atlas privacy policy

Policy version: October 17, 2026

Expo UI Atlas is an independent interactive UI reference app provided by Andrew Bierman through Bierman Collective LLC.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a publisher-operated backend. The publisher does not collect sample text, playground settings, identifiers, or usage activity through the app.

Values entered into sample controls and the configuration editor are used locally in memory. They are not uploaded or synchronized by the app, and are not saved as a user profile. Reset restores the relevant example's starting state. Closing or restarting the app can discard experiments.

## Documentation, links and sharing

Reference text is bundled with the app. Opening external links or loading remotely referenced documentation media can connect to third-party websites, including Expo's documentation hosts. Those hosts may receive normal network information such as an IP address and apply their own privacy policies. The app does not add tracking identifiers to those requests.

If you choose a native sharing example, the operating system sends the displayed sample content to the app or service you select. The publisher does not receive it.

## Support and retention

Support is available by email at [admin@biermancollective.com](mailto:admin@biermancollective.com). Information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and diagnostic details, is used to respond and improve the app. Email providers handle correspondence as part of delivering and storing messages. Do not send passwords or sensitive screenshots. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

The app has no publisher-held account or playground data to delete. To request removal of support correspondence, email the same address without repeating sensitive information.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. For questions, [contact Bierman Collective](mailto:admin@biermancollective.com). The same policy is bundled in the app under Guide → Privacy policy.
