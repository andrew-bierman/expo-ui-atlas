Expo UI Atlas is an independent interactive UI reference app provided by Andrew Bierman.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or a publisher-operated backend. The publisher does not collect sample text, playground settings, identifiers, or usage activity through the app.

Values entered into sample controls and the configuration editor are used locally in memory. They are not uploaded or synchronized by the app, and are not saved as a user profile. Reset restores the relevant example's starting state. Closing/restarting the app can discard experiments.

## Documentation, links and sharing

Reference text is bundled with the app. Opening external links or loading remotely referenced documentation media can connect to third-party websites, including Expo's documentation hosts. Those hosts may receive normal network information such as an IP address and apply their own privacy policies. The app does not add tracking identifiers to those requests.

If you choose a native sharing example, the operating system sends the displayed sample content to the app or service you select. The publisher does not receive it.

## Support and retention

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information in support reports. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. The app has no publisher-held account or playground data to delete.
